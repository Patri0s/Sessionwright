# Code of conduct

Sessionwright is a small project, and its issue tracker and pull requests are a place to work. Everyone taking part is expected to keep it one.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas and code, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Stay on topic: the problem, the proposal, the change.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a request, and that a "no" comes with a reason.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Where it applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and review comments.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the "…" menu of the comment, issue or pull request; GitHub passes the report to the maintainer. For anything that should not be public, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock a conversation, or block an account from the repository, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.
